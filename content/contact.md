## Research interests

DENG Lab studies efficient, reliable generative AI. Current interests include:

- **Efficient language generation** Diffusion language models, parallel decoding, and training, inference, and reinforcement learning systems.
- **Unified multimodal models** Models that connect text, image, and video generation.
- **World models and embodied AI** World modeling, language reasoning, and vision–language–action models.

Explore our [selected research](/#research) and [publications](/publications/) to find work that connects with your interests.

## Prepare your introduction

To help us understand your background, a useful introductory email can include:

1. **Your background** Your current programme or role, institution, and CV.
2. **Your research interests** The research questions you would like to explore and how they relate to our work.
3. **Examples of your work** If available, a link to a project, paper, code repository, or writing sample.

A subject line such as *Prospective student — your name — research interest* can make your message easier to identify.
